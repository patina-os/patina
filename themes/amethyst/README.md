# amethyst

The first patina21 theme: a purple signature palette on a deep aubergine background,
continuing the logo's pink-to-purple gradient. Dark variant.

## Palette

The accent is `base0E` (`#c084fc`), the logo's pink is `base0F` (`#ff5fd0`), the
foreground is a lavender white. The other base16 slots keep their usual meaning (red for
errors, green for strings and so on) so editors and terminals read as expected.

## Wallpapers

Two abstract, generated images, kept as SVG and rendered at 3840x2160 when the theme is
applied:

- `drift.svg` (the default): ribbons of pink and purple light drifting over the dark field
- `bloom.svg`: translucent forms opening out of the dark, lit from pink to purple

The reference for the style is the kind of soft organic visualisation Google DeepMind
publishes on Pexels. Nothing of theirs is used; both files are drawn from scratch with
gradients, blur and a little grain, and are licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0 (`artwork_license`).
The theme data is GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of the repository.
